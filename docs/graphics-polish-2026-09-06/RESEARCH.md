# Corner shading investigation

The current station has room to improve without changing engines or render pipelines. This investigation targeted the user's report of corner shadows changing as the camera turns.

## Findings

The scene uses URP with 28 baked spotlights, 14 baked area lights, one mixed spotlight and one unshadowed realtime point light. The active renderer already uses Forward+. The broad corner darkening was generated by its screen-space ambient occlusion feature, not solely by the baked lightmaps. Disabling SSAO removed that darkening at the same camera position.

Unity describes SSAO as realtime darkening of nearby surfaces, creases and intersections. It uses camera normal/depth information and runs independently from Volume post-processing. This supports testing the renderer feature separately from exposure and bloom. [Unity's SSAO implementation](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/Manual/urp/post-processing-ssao.html).

The original settings were radius 0.7, intensity 1.1 and direct-lighting strength 0.5. The revised settings are 0.18, 0.55 and 0.15. Full-resolution rendering, Depth Normals, high samples, high blur quality and the original noise method are retained. Unity documents radius as the sampling neighbourhood and direct-lighting strength as the visibility of AO on directly lit areas. [Unity's SSAO settings](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/Manual/urp/ssao-renderer-feature-reference.html).

The revised effect leaves narrow contact shading instead of broad black bands. Screen-space AO still has view-dependent limits; this tuning does not make it a world-space shadow solution. Baked lighting continues to provide the room's static lighting. The controlled screenshots establish the source of the broad bands and their reduction; they do not prove that every reported moving-camera glitch has the same cause.

Shadow bias and excessive visible realtime lights are other documented sources of artifacts. The small realtime-light count and the direct SSAO isolation did not justify changing shadow bias or increasing the shadow atlas indiscriminately. [Unity's shadow troubleshooting](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/Manual/urp/shadows-troubleshooting-urp.html).
